Forge It

Turn finished artwork into a print-ready crane kit.
1
Name your kit and set your machine's print DPI (300 is standard for large format).
2
Upload artwork into each panel. Set each panel's real size in inches — pixels update automatically.
3
Edit & shape each panel: open the Edit tray to zoom, rotate, flip, or adjust color. Pick from 14 shapes.
4
Arrange in the preview — drag images to reposition, drag the ⤡ corner to resize, 🔒 to lock a panel you're happy with.
5
Export a ZIP with individual panels, a layout proof, and a print-canvas sheet for your printer.
⌨
Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · Ctrl+S save kit
